Caldence

Caldence plans the recurring meetings of agile roles and teams and exports them to Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar. It is free and open source, and everything stays in your browser: no account, no server, no tracking.

Features

  • Teams, people and roles (Product Owner, Scrum Master, developers) with working hours
  • Recurring meetings: weekly, selected weekdays, every n weeks, monthly, cadence day, sprint or cycle milestone, once
  • Templates for Scrum, Kanban and SAFe, plus your own template files
  • Calendar by week, month or the whole planning horizon, with public and school holidays
  • Load per person and role, warnings when a week is unusually full, optional meeting costs
  • Export and import as .ics files
  • Installable app that works offline; German and English; built for keyboard and screen readers

Caldence needs JavaScript. Please turn it on to use the app.
